The city is not a track.

보도블럭, 다리, 경사, 콘크리트 — 도시의 노면은 불규칙합니다. BR-001은 이 환경을 위해 처음부터 설계되었습니다. 주목받지 않으면서 움직임을 지지하는 신발, quiet performance™.

歩道ブロック、橋、坂道、コンクリート — 都市の路面は不規則です。BR-001はこの環境のためにゼロから設計されました。注目を集めず、動きを支える靴、quiet performance™。

Sidewalks, bridges, slopes, concrete — the city surface is irregular. BR-001 was designed from scratch for this environment. A shoe that supports your movement without drawing attention. quiet performance™.

Bridge Mechanics™

Bridge Mechanics™

Load distribution inspired by bridge engineering. Impact is distributed across the entire structure, not bounced back.
