Wide-Foot-First Design

Most brands design for a standard-width last, then stretch it for "wide" versions. We did the opposite. The BR-001 was built from day one around a wider last — because your feet shouldn't have to conform to the shoe.

•20+ last iterations to perfect the toe box volume and midfoot wrap
•Integrated upper structure distributes pressure evenly — no hot spots
•Inspired by Bridge Mechanics™: the entire upper works as one continuous load-bearing system
•Fits true to size with natural toe splay — no break-in period required
🧱

Competition-Grade Grip

The outsole uses a high-density rubber compound originally developed for mixed urban terrain — concrete, asphalt, gym floors, and everything in between. The multi-directional tread pattern locks into any surface during explosive lateral movements.

•Engineered for box jumps, sled pushes, rope climbs, and lateral shuffles
•Rubber compound tested on concrete, rubber flooring, turf, and wet surfaces
•Flat, ground-aligned geometry ensures maximum surface contact
•500km+ durability testing — no delamination, no wear-through
☁️

Nitrogen-Infused A-TPU

Cheap EVA foam compresses, flattens, and loses energy return within months. Our nitrogen-infused A-TPU (Thermoplastic Polyurethane) foam is harder to manufacture, but it maintains its bounce and structural integrity over thousands of miles. This is the core of our Stable Core™ technology.

•Stable Core™ midsole: consistent support that doesn't degrade over time
•Nitrogen infusion creates micro air cells for superior energy return
•Ground-aligned geometry prevents torsional instability during dynamic movements
•Full marathon tested — same responsiveness at mile 1 and mile 26
⚡

Hybrid Versatility

Most athletes own separate shoes for lifting, running, and cross-training. The BR-001 was engineered as one shoe that handles all three — powered by our Fit in Motion system with the Easy Swap Lace Lock.

•Easy Swap Lace Lock: independent dual-zone lacing adjusts from loose daily wear to locked-in training fit in seconds
•Stable enough for heavy squats and deadlifts
•Responsive enough for treadmill intervals and outdoor runs
•Lateral support for competition-level functional fitness movements
